# Block 11 bring-up case studies

About half of the Block 11 labs are not exercises but the record of getting a modem to work on real hardware. Each records one failure, the experiments that narrowed it down and, eventually, the cause. Read them after the [core path](/zynq-sdr-course/en/lab-track/#route-c--add-the-zynq-board), as worked examples of debugging rather than as procedures to repeat.

## What the cases teach, in one list

- **A counter inside the FPGA is not evidence of a radiated signal.** Only an independent receiver can say that (case 3).
- **A passing test of a neighbouring path proves nothing about the broken one.** A healthy DDS tone hid a disconnected data path (case 3).
- **Check what was actually built before debugging what it does.** A stale block design invalidated weeks of results (case 4).
- **Know where your probe is.** Five labs measured a healthy signal one block before the block that broke it (case 7).
- **Split the failure population before averaging it.** A "BER floor" was two different defects (case 8).
- **One experiment, one hypothesis.** Negative results are results: each eliminated suspect narrowed the search (case 2).
- **Simulation and timing closure are necessary, not sufficient.** A live A/B test has the veto (cases 5 and 6).

## The cases

| # | Labs | Symptom | What it turned out to be | Lesson |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [11.8](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-8-axi-gpreg-bpsk-overlay/)–[11.11](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-11-iio-gpreg-contention-probe/) | The first PL BPSK burst returns no bits; a host IIO capture running at the same time as the burst control fails with a `Bus error` or a refill timeout. | An integration problem around the live PL design, not a tuning problem: disabling the `axi_gpreg` clock-monitor input removed the overlap `Bus error`. The overlay was cut back to a minimal, boot-safe control path first. | When the failure depends on what else runs at the same time, stop tuning gains and look at the structure. Keep a known-good baseline and change one thing at a time. |
| 2 | [11.13](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-13-stock-vs-runtime-rx-compare/)–[11.19](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-19-runtime-bridge-txrx-self-timed-bringup/) | After the runtime overlay is hot-loaded, `tx_valid_count > 0` but `rx_valid_count = 0`, and the host RX capture times out (`errno 110`). | Not the RX common re-init (11.16), not `start_offset` 0–127 (11.17), not host session state (11.18), not TX settle time (11.19). Lab 11.26 later found that the deployed bitstream had been built from an outdated block design. | Each lab ruled out one suspect and said so. That record is what made the real cause findable. |
| 3 | [11.20](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-20-read-rtl-wav-ota-bpsk-ber/)–[11.25](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-25-stock-vs-runtime-dds-tone-sweep/) | An external RTL-SDR decodes the stock-shell BPSK at BER 0, and sees the DDS tone in both stock and runtime modes, but the runtime PL captures sit at BER ≈ 0.35 with no preamble correlation. | The pages first concluded "TX is fine, RX is broken". The data said otherwise: no PL burst was on the air. After the overlay reload the DAC runs in DDS-only mode and ignores the PL stream (found in 11.26). The DDS tone worked because it never used that stream. | `tx_valid_count > 0` proves samples were produced, not transmitted. Labs 11.22–11.25 now carry the correction. |
| 4 | [11.26](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-26-runtime-dds-bypass-bpsk-ota/) | BER stays near 40–50 % on hardware while every simulation passes. | Six defects, found one at a time: the DAC in DDS-only mode; a stale block design whose register map did not match the host (the out-of-context synthesis cache had also hidden RTL fixes); a timing sampler reset only once, so every frame after the first recovered nothing; a wrong sample format; a one-cycle handshake bubble per symbol that under-ran the DAC FIFO and warped time; and a 4-bit preamble lock window that also matched the leading zeros. After the last fix: BER 0 on the chip. | A 50 % BER floor has many possible causes. Fix one, keep a regression test for it, measure again. |
| 5 | [11.33](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-33-residual-cfo-timing-hypothesis/) | A new timing picker passes replay and RTL tests and closes timing. | The live A/B on two boards was worse than the accepted receiver (10–35 % clean attempts against 67.5 %). The candidate was removed. | Simulation passing and timing closure are necessary; the physical A/B decides. |
| 6 | [11.35](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-35-paired-timing-ab/) | A Gardner timing loop helps lock but not clean frames, and collapses at zero CFO. | The cheap error detector used independent signs on I and Q, so its gain depended on the constellation's rotation. A CFO sweeps the rotation and hid the defect; at zero CFO it showed. A dot-product form fixed the invariance. | Test at the condition that removes the masking variable, and report paired results with confidence intervals. |
| 7 | [11.41](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-41-dc-blocker-data-tracking/) | One payload bit is wrong in every flagged frame, although every measurement of the signal at that symbol is healthy. | The capture tap records the input of the receive chain; the DC blocker is the first block after it, and it was following the data. Five labs measured the right quantity in the wrong place. | Know where the probe is. When a measurement and a symptom disagree, the circuit between them is a suspect. |
| 8 | [11.42](/zynq-sdr-course/en/labs/lab-11-42-ber-floor-lock-tolerance/) | After the DC-blocker fix, about 8 % of frames are still dirty. | Two populations: single-bit noise errors, and frames with about half the bits wrong from bit 0, i.e. a whole burst decoded in the wrong rotation because the frame sync locked falsely. | Do not average a bimodal failure into one BER; split it and follow the population that carries the signal. |

The remaining Block 11 labs that are not part of the [core path](/zynq-sdr-course/en/lab-track/#route-c--add-the-zynq-board) (11.9, 11.10, 11.15–11.18, 11.21, 11.23, 11.24) are the individual experiments inside these cases.
